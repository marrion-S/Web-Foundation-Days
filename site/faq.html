Frequently asked questions

What is QuickNotes?

QuickNotes is a simple page for capturing short notes the moment you think of them.

How do I add a note?

Type it into the "New note" field on the Home page and press "Add Note".

Is there a limit to how long a note can be?

Keep notes short and focused. A single sentence or two works best.

How can I share my thoughts about QuickNotes?

Use the feedback form on the About page.
