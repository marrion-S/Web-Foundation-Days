About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple notes app for jotting down ideas, reminders and to-dos the moment they come to mind. It keeps everything on one page so you can capture a thought in seconds and get back to what you were doing.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the Home page and find the "Add a note" form.
  2. Type your idea into the "New note" field.
  3. Press the "Add Note" button to save it.
  4. Review your saved notes in the "Your notes" list.

Features

  • Add a note in a single step
  • See all your notes in one list
  • Live note count so you know how many you have
  • Clean, distraction-free layout

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Add the note you are typing
Tab Move to the next field or button
Shift + Tab Move back to the previous field or button
